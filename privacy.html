Fundamentals Atelier

Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

Fundamentals Atelier is a local app that runs on your computer. This policy covers optional Pinterest access.

Who this applies to

Personal use only. There is no hosted service, no user accounts, and no Fundamentals Atelier server that receives your data.

Information we access

If you connect Pinterest, the app requests read-only access to your account identity, boards you own or collaborate on (including secret boards you allow), and Pins on those boards.

The app does not create, edit, or delete Pins or boards. It does not access other people’s boards unless you are a collaborator. It does not read your Pinterest password. If you never connect Pinterest, none of this applies.

Use and storage

Pinterest data is used only to list your boards and show Pin images in the app. Tokens and cached images stay on your computer. Nothing is sold or shared. Pinterest remains a third party; see Pinterest’s Privacy Policy.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it or disconnect. You can also revoke access in your Pinterest account settings.
