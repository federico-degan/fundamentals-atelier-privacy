Desktop drawing practice

Timed figure drawing,
on your own computer.

Fundamentals Atelier is a local studio tool for gesture and figure practice. You pick reference folders, set a clock, and draw. Nothing is hosted in the cloud.

Your references

Sessions pull images from folders on your machine. Shuffle, loop, and work through a set the way a life-drawing class would.

Practice modes

Classic timed poses, accuracy drills over the reference, drawing from memory, and a staged build-up from gesture to finished figure.

Optional Pinterest

Connect your Pinterest account to use boards you already own — including secret boards — as drawing references. Read-only. Your password is never seen.
