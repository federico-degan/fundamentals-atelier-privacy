Privacy Policy for Gesture Drawing Pro

Last updated: 30 August 2026

Gesture Drawing Pro is a local drawing-practice app that runs on your computer. This policy explains what happens if you connect a Pinterest account so you can use your boards as figure-drawing references.

Who this applies to

This app is for personal use. It is not a hosted service, does not have user accounts of its own, and does not send your data to a Gesture Drawing Pro server.

Information we access

If you choose Connect Pinterest, the app asks Pinterest for read-only access to:

  • your Pinterest account identity (so we know which account is connected)
  • boards you own or collaborate on, including secret boards you explicitly allow
  • Pins on those boards (image URLs and basic pin metadata)

The app does not create, edit, or delete Pins or boards. It does not access other people’s boards unless you are already a collaborator on them. It does not read your Pinterest password.

If you never connect Pinterest, none of this applies. Local reference folders on your computer stay on your computer.

How that information is used

Pinterest data is used only to list your boards and show Pin images as timed drawing references inside the app.

Where it is stored

  • Pinterest access and refresh tokens are stored only on your computer.
  • Pin images may be cached on your computer so sessions can run without re-downloading.
  • Sketches you draw are saved to the folder you choose on your computer.

Nothing from Pinterest is uploaded to Gesture Drawing Pro, sold, or shared with third parties. Pinterest itself remains a third party; their handling of your account is covered by Pinterest’s Privacy Policy.

How long we keep it

Cached images and tokens stay on your computer until you delete them or disconnect Pinterest. Disconnecting stops further Pinterest API access. You can also revoke the app from your Pinterest account settings.
